Carpentry

Plank the holes in the hull: fit the tray's pieces in, and fill whole rows and columns to blast them clear.

Step by stepThe rules in full

Kind
Ship station
Where
The ship's Carpentry station
What for
Seals the holes a battle opens in the hull.

Step by step

  1. Step 1

    Drag a piece onto the hull

    Drag a piece from the tray onto the hull, or click it, then click where it goes. Pieces go in as they come: there's no turning them. A piece that won't fit where it is shows red.

  2. Step 2

    Green means it fits

    Green means it fits, and the lines it would finish light up before you let go.

  3. Step 3

    Fill a row or a column

    Fill a whole row or column and it blasts clear. Finishing several lines with one piece pays far more.

  4. Step 4

    Clear back to back

    Clear on back-to-back moves for a combo, and each clear in the chain pays more than the last.

  5. Step 5

    Use all three, leave room

    Place all three pieces and a fresh set arrives. Leave room for them: if none of your pieces fits anywhere, the hull gives way, the board clears and you lose a quarter of your score. Play as long as you like; every run that scores counts toward your rank.

The rules, as the game tells them

  • DRAG a piece from the tray onto the hull — or click it, then click where it goes
  • Pieces go in as they come: no turning them. Use all three and a fresh set arrives
  • Green = it fits, red = it won't; the lines it would finish light up
  • Fill a whole ROW or COLUMN → it BLASTS clear; clear on back-to-back moves for a combo
  • If none of your pieces fits anywhere, the hull GIVES WAY: the board clears and you lose a quarter of your score

Play as long as you like — every run that scores counts toward your rank; hit ← Leave when you're done.
