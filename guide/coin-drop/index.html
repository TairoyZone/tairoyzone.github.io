Coin Drop

Drop coins down a pegboard of switches and race your rival to the round's target. Best of four rounds.

Step by stepThe rules in full

Kind
Tavern table
Where
The tavern's Coin Drop machine
What for
A duel on a pegboard. Winner takes gold from the loser's daily pocket.

Step by step

  1. Step 1

    Pick a chute and drop

    On your turn, click an entry slot at the top of the board to drop a coin. Or pick a chute with A and D (or the arrow keys) and drop with Space. Your coins are gold; your opponent's are red.

  2. Step 2

    Pads and switches

    A coin rests on an empty pad, bounces off one that's taken, and flips a switch when it crosses the lever side. Whatever reaches the foot of the board drops into a berth and scores the number on it.

  3. Step 3

    Knock coins loose

    Odd flips drop a resting coin off its pad, so one drop can bring others down with it, and bumped coins can merge with falling ones into multi-coins worth that many times the score. Here one drop scores 21, then 13.

  4. Step 4

    Reach the target

    The first to the round's target wins the round: 10 points in round one.

  5. Step 5

    Best of four

    Every round deals its own target and its own berths, and the best of four rounds wins the game. If it stands level after four, round five is sudden death, played with warp holes.

The rules, as the game tells them

  • Click an entry slot at the top to drop a coin (your turn only)
  • Or pick a chute with A / D (or ← →) and drop with SPACE
  • A coin RESTS on an empty pad, BOUNCES off an occupied pad, and FLIPS a switch when it crosses the lever side
  • Odd flips drop the resting coin off the pad — bumped coins can merge with falling ones into multi-coins (xN score)
  • First to the round target wins the round
  • Best of 4 rounds wins the game — and if it stands level, round 5 is sudden death, played with warp HOLES
