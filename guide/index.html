How to play

Every mini-game in Shivaliva, each with its own page: a step-by-step walk through real screenshots, and the rules exactly as they read in play.

On the island

  • Scrub the muck off the tavern's plates: click the instant the circling sponge crosses a spot, and keep your flow going before the hot water runs cold.

    How it plays

  • Stack the falling pairs of wood into squares of one kind. A square is a slab, and slabs are what pay.

    How it plays

  • Turn the rock face four stones at a time, crumble runs of one colour, and dig the ore chunks down into the cart.

    How it plays

  • Texas Hold'em at the tavern table, for the islanders' own gold. Your chips are gold, one for one.

    How it plays

  • Drop coins down a pegboard of switches and race your rival to the round's target. Best of four rounds.

    How it plays

  • Falling blocks, head to head: clear lines to bury your opponent in garbage and steel until their board tops out.

On the ship

  • Bat an ember of Stardust off your coal shovel into the coal face of the ship's firebox, and keep her boiler drawing.

    How it plays

  • Turn the ship's wheel to shoot drift-rock into the field above. Break three of a colour, and whatever is left hanging falls.

    How it plays

  • Hit each charge on the beat as it reaches its gun: a bucket, powder, wad and shot, and the gun fires.

    How it plays

  • Plank the holes in the hull: fit the tray's pieces in, and fill whole rows and columns to blast them clear.

    How it plays

  • Swap stones to line up three of a hue, and vent the Stardust that seeps into the hold.

  • Plan four moves and your guns at the table while the raider plans hers, then watch both play out on the grid.
