Dish Duty

The tavern's kitchen sink. Pays gold per plate. Ungated: no tools, no fee, and the one job a broke player can always reach.

  • The sponge circles the plate — CLICK / SPACE the instant it crosses a caked-on MUCK spot
  • Grime is crusted on in LAYERS — the further into the rack, the more clean scrubs a spot takes
  • Every good scrub flips the sponge's direction — stay sharp
  • Nail scrubs back-to-back to build your FLOW: faster sweeps, tighter timing, MORE suds
  • A scrub that doesn't finish a lump THROWS it on round the rim — sometimes clear to the far side — so be ready to chase it
  • A mistimed scrub SLIPS: your flow breaks, grime smears back where you slipped, and THIS plate CHIPS
  • 3 chips on one plate and it SHATTERS — no pay, and a strike. 3 strikes and the cook sends you off — this sitting is over
  • A plate that makes it to the rack leaves its chips behind — only a SHATTERED plate costs you
  • The HOT WATER drains as you work — let it run cold and the shift ends there
  • Scrub a KETTLE off a plate's rim and it tops the water back up — worth going out of your way for
  • Pay CLIMBS the longer you stay: 1g for your first plates, 6g once you're rolling
  • Finish the whole rack for +10g. Wash 6 or more in ONE sitting without smashing a single plate for +15g on top of that
  • Faster? hold RIGHT-CLICK / SHIFT. One load a day — and leaving early restarts the pay ladder

Lumberjacking

The Grove work-site. Pays wood, sold at the Market.

  • ← → move the falling pair · ↑ or Z rotate ccw · C rotate cw · ↓ or SPACE drop faster (hold)
  • Pack a 2×2 or bigger SQUARE of one wood — that square is a SLAB, and slabs are the only thing that pays wood
  • A long line of matching wood pays NOTHING — build squares, not rows
  • Land a matching AXE against a group and the whole group SHATTERS, paying out every slab in it
  • Chain shatters back-to-back for combo score — and a chain cuts MORE wood: a Double's slabs pay half again, a Triple's twice over
  • Every shift (the hourglass) pays gold for the SCORE you made in it, the moment it ends
  • A KNOT won't clear while it's a knot — but it ROTS: it warms from grey to amber, and three drops after it lands it turns into a plain log (sometimes an axe)
  • Let the pile reach the top and the chute jams — your time at the saw ends (the shift you were on is still paid)

Mining

The mine work-site. Pays ore, sold at the Forge.

  • Move the 2x2 cursor with the arrow keys or the mouse
  • Rotate it: C / right-click = clockwise, X / left-click = counter-clockwise
  • Line up 3+ of the same color to crumble that rock
  • Clear the rock UNDER an ore chunk to dig it down to the floor — chunks are the only thing that scores
  • Dig several chunks in one move for a combo bonus
  • Big clears drop a TOOL — frame it (the cursor shrinks to 1x1) and click to use it
  • Light every pip on the chunk meter beside the board (one per chunk you dig) to finish the seam
  • The faster you dig the quota out, the more the seam wage pays — up to double (the SPEED PAY counter)
  • The rarer the stone, the more Cinder Tor pays for it: ore, then emerald, sapphire, and diamond

Poker

The tavern's card table. Texas Hold'em against the islanders, for real gold out of their own pockets.

  • Click action buttons (Fold / Check / Call / Bet / Raise / All-In)
  • Slide the bet amount on raises
  • Best 5-card hand from your 2 hole cards + 5 community cards wins
  • Your chips ARE gold (1:1) — buy in, then Cash Out when you Leave
  • You cash out the chips still in front of you, so chips lost are gold lost
  • A WIN is only ever as big as the money the others still have on them — beat a table of paupers and there is little there to take
  • Bust and you walk away with nothing

Coin Drop

The tavern's Coin Drop machine. A duel on a pegboard. Winner takes gold from the loser's daily pocket.

  • Click an entry slot at the top to drop a coin (your turn only)
  • Or pick a chute with A / D (or ← →) and drop with SPACE
  • A coin RESTS on an empty pad, BOUNCES off an occupied pad, and FLIPS a switch when it crosses the lever side
  • Odd flips drop the resting coin off the pad — bumped coins can merge with falling ones into multi-coins (xN score)
  • First to the round target wins the round
  • Best of 4 rounds wins the game — and if it stands level, round 5 is sudden death, played with warp HOLES

Skirmish

The Cradle Gym, the Labyrinth, and any boarding. Falling blocks, head to head. The island's whole fighting ladder runs on it.

  • ← → : move the piece
  • ↑ / Z : rotate
  • ↓ : soft drop (hold)
  • SPACE : HARD drop — it commits the piece
  • TAB : HOLD / swap the piece
  • F : your SIGNATURE MOVE, once its badge glows gold

Clear lines to send GARBAGE to your opponent. Fill their board to the top to win.

Incoming attacks land as inert grey blocks that clog your stack — they can't be cleared until they RIPEN into coloured tiles (after a couple of your drops).

You BOTH have an HP bar above your profile. A multi-row volley that lands is a BLOW: it chips HP *and* raises that many courses of unbreakable STEEL from the bottom of their board.

DIG BACK OUT: every line you clear above the floor CHIPS the top steel course, and enough chips break it off. FASTER — each course carries one gold KEYHOLE tile: cover it with a piece and clear the line it sits in, and that whole course cracks off in one go.

But the more WORN you are, the more clears each course takes — and at 0 HP you cannot dig at all, so the floor only ever rises. The line under your name says WORN when that starts costing you.

You never lose from HP alone. You lose ONLY by topping out — so damage does not kill you, it takes away your way back up.

THE MYSTIC IS THE HEALER: they knit HP back when they crack a course off their own floor, and their Mend restores more than anyone else's. Since a worn fighter digs worse, healing buys back both the damage AND the ability to dig out of it — the trade for having the weakest Strike on the ladder.

YOUR SIGNATURE MOVE: clearing lines fills the badge under your HOLD box. When it is full and glowing gold, press F to unleash it — your style decides what it does, and the foe has one too. It costs nothing but the clears you earned, so it is always available to both of you.

YOUR WEAPON matters here: Strike chips them harder, Guard means their blows chip you less, Armour is plates over your bar that break first, Tempo gives you more time to place each piece, and Venom keeps your garbage unclearable longer. A weapon swings a blow by about a third either way; it never sends more garbage, so it can shorten a fight you were winning and cannot win one for you. This is a friendly spar — no real HP is lost.

Stoking

The ship's Stoking station. Feeds her boiler on a voyage.

  • The ball of Stardust is an EMBER. Your shovel is the bar at the foot — A / D or drag to point it
  • Catch embers and she draws; every one you miss is a push she never gets
  • ★ WHERE the ember strikes the shovel sets where it goes — centre sends it back steep and slow, the outer edge sends it flat and FAST. That is your angle on the coal, and it is the whole craft
  • Punch a lane clean through the coal face and the ember gets a straight run behind it
  • Miss and the ASH banks up from the grate: your pressure falls and the shovel itself goes sluggish
  • The coal never runs out — clear a face and the next one rolls in, in a new shape
  • The notches left of the board are your LEVEL. Stoke well and new coal opens; lose embers and it falls back

THE COAL

  • Colour is TOUGHNESS: red is one hit, then blue, green, yellow, pink, black, striped, rainbow, and the brown shell is the hardest thing in the firebox. Every hit knocks a block down one colour, so you can always see how much is left in it
  • FIREDAMP goes up and takes everything next to it — aim for the middle of a seam, not the edge
  • SPARK SWARMS drift along their row and will not hold still
  • FIREBRICKS never break. Learn to bank the ember off them
  • BLAST PIPES never break either, but they pay you and throw the ember out faster
  • SMOKE gathers BACK if you leave it. Clear it last
  • Some coal lies buried in the ash until something touches it

BROKEN COAL DROPS THINGS — CATCH THESE

  • SPARKS FLY! — The ember splits in three.
  • BROAD SHOVEL — A broader shovel.
  • DAMPED DOWN — The ember eases off.
  • FIRE TONGS — The ember sticks. Space to let it go.
  • POKERS — Space throws a poker from the shovel.
  • BLAZE — The ember burns straight through.
  • FIRE GRATE — One ember saved at the foot.
  • RAKE THE ASH — Clears a layer of ash.
  • GOOD DRAUGHT — Pressure, free.

⚠️ AND DODGE THESE

  • BLOWBACK — The ember runs away with you.
  • SMALL SHOVEL — Your shovel is cut down.
  • CROSS-DRAUGHT — Left is right.
  • THICK SMOKE — You stoke on memory.

Navigation

The ship's Navigation station. Steers the leg. A good watch multiplies what the sailors earn; a booch costs you course.

You are at the wheel. Turn it to lay the shot over, and send drift-rock into the field above.

Three of a colour touching breaks them — and anything left hanging with nothing above it falls, which is worth far more than the three you broke.

HOLD YOUR FIRE and the wheel winds up: that shot BANKS its group instead of breaking it, and the held rock sits there glowing and breathing together. Break anything touching a bank and the whole run goes off at once, with a blast that takes the rock around it. Bigger run, bigger blast.

The sky comes down a row on a clock (the seconds beside the board count down to it), and sooner as the colours run out. Let the rock reach the bottom row and you have BOOCHED. At sea she turns about and re-sails the leg — it costs you course, never your hull. Ashore the round is dealt again, and the third booch ends the watch.

Turn the wheel: A / D, the D-pad, or just point.

Fire: Space.

Hold fire: Shift.

Gunnery

The ship's Gunnery station, in the hold. Loads the cannons. What you load on a leg is the shot the ship has to fire when a raider catches her.

The deck plays a song. Hit each piece as it crosses its gun's ring, on the beat. Hold the key through a glowing trail until it ends.

Your ship has 4 guns, and every piece that comes down a lane is the one its gun needs next - a bucket to swab it out, then powder, wad, shot. The fourth goes in and the gun fires; guns that fire together are a broadside.

Every ten hits in a row raise your multiplier, up to x4. A miss, or a press with nothing there, breaks the run - and miss too many and the Gun Captain sends you off the deck.

Hit every gilded piece of a run to gather Stardust (from level 3); at half a meter, unleash it to double your multiplier.

Guns: A S D and J K L (the D-pad and shoulders on a pad). Unleash Stardust: Shift.

Carpentry

The ship's Carpentry station. Seals the holes a battle opens in the hull.

  • DRAG a piece from the tray onto the hull — or click it, then click where it goes
  • Pieces go in as they come: no turning them. Use all three and a fresh set arrives
  • Green = it fits, red = it won't; the lines it would finish light up
  • Fill a whole ROW or COLUMN → it BLASTS clear; clear on back-to-back moves for a combo
  • If none of your pieces fits anywhere, the hull GIVES WAY: the board clears and you lose a quarter of your score

Play as long as you like — every run that scores counts toward your rank; hit ← Leave when you're done.

Venting

The ship's Venting station. Clears the Stardust haze that seeps in through those holes.

  • CLICK a stone, then click a neighbour above, below or beside it — or DRAG it onto one.
  • Arrow keys move the cursor; SPACE picks a stone up and puts it down.
  • EVERY swap is allowed, even one that matches nothing — spend moves setting a combo up.
  • Line up 3+ of a hue in a row or column to ignite them. Every clear breathes clean air into the hold.
  • Clear MORE THAN ONE line in a SINGLE swap (a combo: Spark! / Aurora! / Firstlight!) for big LIFT.
  • THE STARDUST seeps in through the hull and hangs in the air, thicker every move. Clear stones to vent it; dawdle on weak clears and it fills the hold until she's overcome.

THE PUMP BESIDE THE HULL IS YOUR GRADE. It reads your last 10 swaps — lift banked per swap — and works faster and glows brighter the better you vent; hover it for the word the duty report will give you. The lantern in the hold burns that same colour.

YOUR LEVEL is the bar down the left. Fly Good or better and it climbs; fly Poor and it falls back. It remembers where you settled, so the board finds the level that challenges YOU. Each level adds something to the board and raises what your best combo is worth (×2 at the bottom, uncapped at the top).

  • BELLOWS (level 2) — a pale sack that drifts in. Press it and it blows its own nine. It banks NO lift; what it buys is the cascade that falls in after, and air against the Stardust.
  • GALE (level 3) — match FOUR in a line. The stone earns a double arrow; match it again and the wind clears the whole line the arrow points along.
  • BALLAST (level 4) — an iron-banded crate lands on the board and stays put. You can't swap or match it, and stones slide round it, but every clear BESIDE it snaps a band off. When the last band goes it's thrown OVERBOARD for big LIFT (more the thicker the Stardust) and the air clears. A Bellows blast beside it counts.
  • BURST (level 5) — match an L or a T. The stone earns a star; match it again and it blasts everything around it.
  • NOVA (level 6, and dealt from level 7) — match FIVE. A dark stone with a star in it: swap it onto any stone and every stone of that colour ignites.

Space Battle

On a voyage, when a raider catches you. Ship against ship in a box that closes in. Grapple her to board, and the damage you did buries her crew before the boarding starts.

Plan four moves, then press Ready — or the glass runs out and whatever you planned sails. The raider plans at the same moment, and you do not see her moves.

Each phase: your move, then the stardust currents push one square and the whirls swing you round, then the guns fire. Forward sails one square; left or right sails one forward and one to that side, and turns you.

HOP leaps her off the board for one phase: she keeps her square, and broadsides and grapples aimed at her that phase pass underneath — but in the air she cannot fire or grapple. Her sailors make a hop only every other turn, so save it for the broadside you see coming.

The table is your ship, bow up: a well for each phase down her keel, and a gun-port on each side of it — the ports on her left are her port guns. Click a move tile to lay it in the lit well (or drag it to any well). Click a gun-port to load a ball there; guns fire straight out of that side, three squares.

Drop the GRAPPLE on a gun-port to throw a hook from that side: if she is right alongside after your move, it bites and you board her. You never run out of grapples, but no guns fire in a phase you throw one. A port glows gold where she would be alongside, red (with a gunsight) where she is in that broadside's lane — if she holds her course.

Your gunners make the shot and your sailors make the moves (the crew on the right of the table); a move tile you do not use goes off after five turns, and the dot over a tile picks the kind they make next. Damage you do buries her crew before the boarding starts — and the damage she does buries yours.

The box closes in from turn 4; the thin line shows where its wall will be next. Its walls stop you and hurt. You may disengage after 10 turns — every ball that hits you adds 2 more.

Moves: W A D or the D-pad. Hop: H or the left shoulder. Clear: S. Guns: Q / E or the triggers. Grapple: G.

Next phase: Tab. Ready: Space.

Look around: drag, or the right stick. Zoom: the wheel.
