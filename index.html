Twelve mini-games

Every job is a game

Every job on the island, every table in the tavern, every station on the ship and every fight is its own mini-game. Point at one to see it played. How each one plays.

Arrive with nothing

You arrive on Cradle Rock with nothing. Take jobs that are each their own mini-game, play the inn's tables and climb the five-rung gym ladder. Then take to the sky, where every station aboard is another mini-game: sign on with the ferry's crew, or buy a ship and hire your own. Across the sky on Driftspar stands the Labyrinth, the first of the Ordeals, and its beasts wait one at a time. Buy a lot and the shipwright builds you a house of your own. None of it ends: keep playing for as long as you like. How each mini-game plays.

The ladder, then the Labyrinth

Five friendly bouts at the Cradle Gym, easiest first. Hollow Sable runs the ladder and Mossy Senna patches you up after. Beat them all and you are Gym Champion.

Then the Labyrinth's gate on Driftspar opens, across the sky. Five beasts, one at a time, up to the Labyrinth King. Lose, and you wake back at the Cradle Gym with the healer.

Hearties and rivals

Rapport runs from Despised to Confidant. Earn it and they act like it, in what they say and what they will do for you.

And if the feeling runs both ways, court them: fond, then smitten, then sweethearts.

The islanders have lives of their own

You can play the whole game without saying a word to anyone. Stop and talk anyway.

  • Ask Bralo what the word is tonight

    He tells you about the room you are both standing in: who just won at Coin Drop, who is hiring at the forge, who has been spinning tall tales by the fire all evening.

  • Win at the gym, and the tavern already knows

    Beat someone in a friendly bout at the Cradle Gym and by the time you walk into the inn the whole cast has heard. Lose, and that goes round too.

  • Ask anyone to walk with you

    They weigh how much they like you, whether they are busy, and how far off you are. Then they fall in beside you, or tell you no and why.

Conversations need a live internet connection. Everything else in the game plays offline.

Where it's going

  1. Playable now

    Cradle Rock and Driftspar

    Cradle Rock, fully dressed: its jobs and tables to play, a cast who keep their own schedules and remember you, and the gym ladder. Then Driftspar, across the sky, and the Labyrinth, the first Ordeal.

  2. Playable now

    A ship and a crew

    Buy a hull at the sky dock, hire a crew and sail out. Every duty station that keeps her flying is a mini-game of its own, and a voyage can end in a battle in the sky and a boarding.

  3. Playable now

    A home of your own

    Buy a lot, and the shipwright builds you a house to furnish as you like. Take someone you are sweet on home with you.

  4. Planned

    The Ordeals, and the secret island

    Eight Ordeals, each on an island of its own, each with a master waiting at the end. Every one you beat gives you a bearing on an island nobody has charted.

  5. Planned

    More to play

    The mini-games here are where it starts, not where it stops. More work to take on, more games on the tavern tables, and some that belong to one island and nowhere else. New ones keep coming rather than arriving once.

  6. Planned

    A sky full of islands

    A whole chart of them, each one built by hand with its own cast and its own tables, scattered across the sky at their own heights. How far your ship can carry you is what opens the map up.

No dates on purpose. Art and audio are placeholder for now.

Questions

  • Is Shivaliva free?

    Yes. The Beta Demo is free on itch.io for Windows, name your own price, no sign-up.

  • Does the demo end?

    No. The Beta Demo is a sandbox. Your first crossing to Driftspar earns Off the Rock, and then you keep playing for as long as you like. The full game is a new journey of its own.

  • What kind of game is it?

    A single-player life-sim where every job is a puzzle, on islands floating in space. The twelve mini-games are the island's jobs, the tavern's tables, the ship's duty stations and the fights: Dish Duty, Lumberjacking, Mining, Poker, Coin Drop, Skirmish, Stoking, Navigation, Gunnery, Carpentry, Venting and Space Battle.

  • Do I need an internet connection?

    Only for conversations with the islanders. Everything else in the game plays offline.

  • Is it multiplayer?

    No. Shivaliva is a full single-player experience.

  • Who makes it?

    Trojan Bulldog, one person, building in public in Godot 4.7. The devlog is on itch and updates are posted on X as @trojanatoplat.
