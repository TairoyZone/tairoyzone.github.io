SHORT DESCRIPTION
Shivaliva is a single-player puzzle-skill adventure among floating sky-islands.
Gather your crew, defeat the Ordeals and find the secret island. Every job, card
table and ship's station is its own mini-game, and so is every fight.

THE DEMO
You start on Cradle Rock with nothing. Take jobs that are each their own
mini-game, play the tavern's tables, climb the five-rung Cradle Gym ladder, then
walk into the Labyrinth, the first of the Ordeals. Then buy a ship at the sky
dock, hire a crew and sail out: every duty station aboard is another mini-game,
and a voyage can end in a battle in the sky and a boarding.

Twelve mini-games. On the island: Mining, Coin Drop, Lumberjacking, Dish Duty,
Skirmish, Poker. On the ship: Sailing, Navigation, Gunnery, Carpentry, Venting.
And when a raider catches you: the Space Battle. Character creator. One
currency, shop, backpack, chart. Islanders worth talking to: they remember what
you did, know what is happening in the room, and pass word about you across the
island.

Conversations with islanders need a live internet connection. Everything else
plays offline.

PLANNED
Eight Ordeals, each on an island of its own with a master at the end, every one
a bearing on the secret island. More mini-games, and drop-in co-op for a few
friends.
