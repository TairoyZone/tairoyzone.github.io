SHORT DESCRIPTION
Shivaliva Shanty is a single-player puzzle-skill adventure set among floating
sky-islands. Four of its six mini-games are jobs on the island; the other two
are the tavern's tables. Every islander you meet answers you in their own voice,
knows what is going on in the room, and remembers what you did.

THE DEMO
One island, Cradle Rock. You arrive as nobody, take four jobs that are each
their own mini-game, play the tavern's two tables, climb the five-rung Cradle
Gym ladder and face the Labyrinth Ordeal, reached from the Cradle Gym. The demo
ends when you buy passage off the island at the skydock.

Six mini-games: Mining, Coin Drop, Lumberjacking, Dish Duty, Skirmish, Poker.
Ten islanders who remember what you did, know what is happening in the room, and
pass word about you across the island. Character creator. One currency, shop,
backpack, chart.

Conversations with islanders need a live internet connection. Everything else
plays offline.

AFTER THE DEMO (not in the demo)
Ships and voyages, boarding and sea battle, a second island (Driftspar), courting
islanders, a house you can buy and furnish, and four ship duty-station puzzles
(Lofting, Patchworks, Sailing, Starfix).
