Description

Short description

Shivaliva is a single-player life-sim where every job is a puzzle. Live on a floating island in space, play well to earn gold, and spend it on new looks, a home or your own ship. Make friends, or more, with islanders who remember you, then sail the stars, where even the fights are puzzle duels.

The demo starts you on Cradle Rock with nothing. You take work at the mine, the grove, the tavern sink and the coin table, and each job is a different puzzle with its own rules. You sit down at the inn's poker table. You walk into the Cradle Gym and climb its five-rung ladder in Skirmish, a two-board versus block puzzle where your cleared lines land on your rival's floor. Topping the ladder opens the Labyrinth, the first of the Ordeals, which stands across the sky on Driftspar, and its beasts wait one at a time. To get there you sign on with the ferry's crew, or buy a ship at the sky dock and hire your own. Every duty station aboard is another mini-game, and a voyage can end in a battle in the sky and a boarding. Nothing ends: the Beta Demo is a sandbox, and you can buy a lot and have the shipwright build you a house.

The islanders are the thing players find on their own. Nobody has to talk to anyone, but walk up to any of them and they know where they are standing, what they are doing, and who just won at the table. They remember your last conversation the next day. Word about you travels: win at the gym and the barkeep has heard about it before you reach the bar. Needle a fighter about their record and they may challenge you for it.

It is made by one person in Godot: a world of floating islands, the Stardust you sail through between them, and a crew of your own.

Conversations with islanders need a live internet connection. Everything else in the game plays offline.

Features

What the Beta Demo contains.

  • Cradle Rock, Driftspar, and a ship to crew. A small island with a tavern, a forge, a market, a gym, a mine, a grove and a sky dock, and a rock across the sky where the Labyrinth stands. Sign on with the ferry's crew, or buy a ship at the dock, hire a crew and sail out.
  • Twelve mini-games. On the island: Mining (a forage grid carved into rock), Coin Drop (the tavern's coin table, a pegboard the coins bounce down), Lumberjacking (a log frame in the grove), Dish Duty (the tavern sink), Skirmish (a two-board versus block puzzle: clear lines and steel lands on your rival's floor) and Poker (the inn's table, ten stools round it). On the ship: Stoking (batting an ember off the coal shovel into the coal face of her firebox, to keep her boiler drawing), Navigation (a bubble shooter at the wheel that steers the leg), Gunnery (ramming powder, wad and shot into the cannons as the deck brings them down), Carpentry (sealing the holes a battle opens in the hull) and Venting (clearing the Stardust haze out of the hold). And when a raider catches you: the Space Battle (ship against ship in a box that closes in, with a grapple to board her).
  • The Cradle Gym ladder, then the first Ordeal. Five rungs of Skirmish against named islanders, then the Labyrinth Ordeal on Driftspar and its beasts, one at a time.
  • Character creator. Every option shown as a portrait: 14 faces, 38 haircuts, 18 beards, brows, scars and war paint. You start in plain clothes and buy the rest.
  • One currency. Gold. A shop that shows the item itself, not an icon, dyes you buy once and keep, a backpack, and a chart of the island.
  • Islanders worth talking to. Optional, and easy to miss. Each knows the room they are standing in, what they are doing, and what just happened. They remember you between conversations, and they pass word about you across the island.
  • Hearties, and sweethearts. Rapport runs from Despised to Confidant, and it changes what people say and what they will do for you. Put someone on your hearties roster or your crew, and if the feeling runs both ways, court them: fond, then smitten, then sweethearts.
  • A home of your own. Buy a lot, the shipwright builds the house, and you furnish it.
  • No ending. The Beta Demo is a sandbox: once you have crossed to Driftspar, keep playing for as long as you like.
  • Everything drawn in 3D. Every board is a room with objects in it, and the rules underneath are unchanged.
  • Windows, free, no sign-up. About 421 MB.

Planned

The full game goes past Cradle Rock: eight Ordeals, each on an island of its own with a master waiting at the end, and every one you beat is a bearing on the secret island. More mini-games along the way.
