What helps me fix it

  1. What happened, and what you expected to happen instead.
  2. Where you were and what you were doing: the island or the ship, the mini-game, the person you were talking to, the last thing you clicked.
  3. Can you make it happen again? If you can, the steps that do it.
  4. Your version. It is printed in the corner of the title screen (and above, when you came from the game's pause menu).
  5. A screenshot or a short clip, if you have one.
  6. The game's log file. Paste %APPDATA%\Shivaliva\logs into the address bar of File Explorer and attach godot.log. Send it soon after the bug: the game starts a new log each time it opens.
